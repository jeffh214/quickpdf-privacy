QuickPDF privacy policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

QuickPDF is an offline utility for PDFs and photos. This policy describes what the app does with information on your phone.

Data the app does not collect

QuickPDF does not collect, store on a server, or share personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, and no advertising. The developer does not receive your files, your name, your location, or a copy of what you edit.

PDFs and photos you pick are read and written on this device only. Merged, split, compressed, converted, resized, and cleaned files stay on the phone unless you choose to save or share them yourself.

Purchases

QuickPDF Pro is an optional one-time purchase. Google Play processes the payment. QuickPDF does not see your card or payment details. Google Play tells the app whether this Google account owns the product quickpdf_pro. That check is between the app and Google Play on the device.

Permissions

The app asks to open files you select. On Android 8 and 8.1 it may ask for storage permission so it can save into Downloads. Newer Android versions use the system Downloads collection and do not need that permission. The app does not request location, contacts, camera, or microphone access.
